About Hervin Ramirez

One owner, one crew, one phone number.

We're a deck and fence contractor working out of Adairsville, GA. Hervin Ramirez owns the company and answers his own phone at (706) 879-8873. Not a dispatcher, not an answering service.

Most people find us looking for a deck builder, and decks are a good part of what we do. The truck says deck and fence contractor, and even that leaves things out. Porches, retaining walls, concrete, grading. If it goes in the back yard and something has to hold weight, it's probably us.

Meet the owner

Hervin spent three years at Adolfo V. Hall, a military academy in Guatemala, and by his second year he was leading a platoon of fellow cadets. That's responsibility for other people at an age when most of us are still being handed it. School vacations went to job sites with his father, in construction and grading, and that's where the interest in building things from the ground up started.

After coming to the United States he worked as an industrial mechanic at Shaw, from 2015 to 2019. Machinery, troubleshooting, and the habit of doing a job correctly the first time, because the alternative is doing it twice. Ramirez Services Unlimited grew out of all of it.

Today we build custom decks, Trex and composite decking, covered and screened porches, fencing, retaining walls, grading and outdoor construction across north Georgia and metro Atlanta. Hervin is on the jobs himself. Ask why and you'll get a short answer: that's what it means to put your name on something.

What happens between the first call and the last board

Most of the worry in hiring a contractor isn't really about price. It's about not knowing what's supposed to happen next.

  1. You call, or you send the details. A photo of the spot answers more questions than a paragraph does, so send one if you have it.
  2. We come and stand in it. Slope, drainage, access for a truck, what the ground is actually made of. None of that reads off a screen.
  3. You talk through the options. Material, size, where the steps land, which way the boards run. This is the part where money is saved or wasted.
  4. You get a number for the job. Scope first, then the figure, so you can see what the figure is buying.
  5. We build it. The crew that quoted it is the crew on site. Nobody runs it from a phone.
  6. You walk it together at the end. Anything that's not right gets put right before we leave.
