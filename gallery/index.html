His own jobs

Completed work

Every photograph that goes on this page is a job this crew built. None of it is bought in, and none of it is a manufacturer's catalog shot.

The photographs are still coming off his phone

He has been building for customers far longer than he has been photographing it for a website.

The pictures exist. They are on a phone, in message threads and on his own accounts.

They are being collected job by job rather than dumped in a batch.

Until they are here, these are his:

  • Instagram, which is mostly decking.
  • Facebook, which is where he posts a finished job.
  • The Google profile, which carries both photographs and the reviews.

What goes on this page, and what does not

Three rules, and they are the reason this page is thin rather than full.

  • His work only. No stock photography, no supplier images, nothing from a catalog.
  • Before and after from the same angle. A pair shot from two different corners of the yard is a comparison you cannot actually make.
  • Labeled by town, material and how long it took. The duration is the number almost nobody in this trade publishes.

A before photograph cannot be reconstructed after the fact.

Where there is no real before, the after stands on its own and is labeled as one.

Why it is worth waiting for

Ten competitors were looked at across this market.

Not one of them puts a customer's own words next to a photograph of that customer's job.

Stars are decoration. A quote sitting beside the deck it describes is evidence.

That is what this page is being built to hold.

In the meantime, the reviews are the part of the record that is already public.

What he built

The services page covers each family in detail: decks, fencing, pergolas and patio covers, screened porches, retaining walls, patios and walkways, and fire pits.
