Before you call anybody

Guides and ideas

Most of what a back yard project costs is decided before a contractor is ever called. These are the decisions that do the deciding.

A builder can only quote what you ask for.

Ask for the wrong thing and you get an accurate price for the wrong thing.

So these pages are about the fork in the road, not the finish line.

Wood or composite. Wall or regrade. Screened porch, or an open patio with shade over it.

They are also the only pages here where money appears, and that is deliberate.

A number without the reasoning behind it is worth nothing to you.

Read these first

The guides

Planning a whole yard

Backyard Ideas on a Slope

A sloped backyard is not a design problem. It is a sequence problem. Build the pieces in the wrong order and you pay for the first one twice.

Decks

Deck Design Ideas

Design ideas for a deck fall into two piles: the ones that change how it looks and the ones that change how it has to be built. Knowing which is which before you call is worth money.

Decks

Deck Size and Board Calculator

Work out the size first. Once you know the square footage, the board count and how many joists sit under it, every quote you receive becomes readable.

Fire pits

Fire Pit Ideas and Designs

A fire pit is the cheapest thing you can add to a yard that changes how often people are in it. Getting it right is mostly about size, position and what you sit on.

Deck care

How to Stain a Deck in Georgia

Most deck finishes in north Georgia fail early for one of two reasons: the boards were not dry, or they were not clean. Neither has anything to do with which can you bought.

Patios, walkways and steps

Patio and Walkway Ideas

A patio is a surface that will not absorb rain, and a walkway is a line across a grade. Both of them change where water goes. Design them knowing that and they age well.

Pergolas and patio covers

Pergola Ideas and Designs

A pergola is the cheapest way to make a hot patio usable and the most misunderstood structure in the backyard. Here is what it does, what it will not do, and how to pick a design before you call anybody.

Fencing

Privacy Fence Ideas and Styles

Fence styles look like a taste decision and are mostly a structural one. Every pattern below changes what the wind does to the fence, and therefore what the posts have to be.

Retaining walls

Retaining Walls Compared

Most retaining wall failures are drainage failures wearing a different hat. Start with what goes behind the wall, then pick the material, then worry about how it looks.

Porches and enclosures

Screened Porch Ideas

The difference between a porch that gets used every evening and one that becomes storage is decided by four things, and three of them are settled before anybody picks a paint color.

Choosing

Types of Fences and Decking

Every outdoor project starts with the same four arguments: which fence, which decking, deck or patio, and porch or sunroom. Here they are, side by side, with the part people find out too late.

Fencing

What a Fence Costs

Fence quotes for the same yard routinely differ by a factor of two. Almost none of that gap is profit. It is gates, posts, terrain and what happens to the old fence.

Patios, walkways and steps

What a Paver Patio Costs

Two quotes for the same patio, one noticeably cheaper. Nine times out of ten the difference is not the pavers. It is how much of the ground came out before they went down.

Porches and enclosures

What a Screened Porch Costs

Screened porch prices look wildly inconsistent online because three completely different jobs share the same name. Work out which one you are buying and the numbers stop being confusing.

Fencing

Wood Fence Maintenance

A wood fence has one clock on it, and it runs at the ground line. Everything below is either about slowing that clock down or about what to do once it has run out.
