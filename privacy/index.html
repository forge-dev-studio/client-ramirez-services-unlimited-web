Plain version

Privacy

Short, because there is not much to say. One form, one recipient, and nothing sold to anybody.

What the form collects

The estimate form asks for your name, a phone number, your town, what you are building and roughly how big it is.

The note is optional and marked as such. If you have a photo of the spot, it goes by text to the same phone that receives the form, and this site never stores it.

There is no email field, no budget field and no date picker, because none of the three help Hervin Ramirez call you back.

Who reads it

Hervin Ramirez does. It reaches him so he can call you about the job you described.

It is not sold, rented, traded or handed to a lead broker. Nobody buys their way into that list.

How long it is kept

As long as the job conversation is live, and after that as an ordinary business record.

Ask us to delete it and we will. Call (706) 879-8873 or email ramirezservices87@gmail.com and say so.

Calls and texts

Sending the form is an invitation for one call back about that job. It is not a subscription to anything.

We don't run a marketing list, and nothing here signs you up for one.

Measurement

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. It does not identify you by name.

Things loaded from elsewhere

The typeface comes from Google Fonts, so Google's servers see the request your browser makes for it. That and the Google Analytics tag are the two third party assets on the page.

Links out to Google, Facebook and Instagram are ordinary links. Once you follow one you are on their site and under their terms, not this one's.

The contact page has the hours and the rest of the detail.

Questions about any of this?

Ask us directly. The same number answers for privacy as for a deck.
