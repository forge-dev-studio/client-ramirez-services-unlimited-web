Deck building and deck repair in Alpharetta: 1996, and an original deck near thirty

Pergolas and patio covers in Alpharetta: 65.1 percent owner occupied

Screened porches in Alpharetta: 39 lakes and ponds inside the city

The Census Bureau's water maps show 39 lakes and ponds inside Alpharetta, 235 acres of water in all. The largest covers about 171 acres, and most of the rest are ponds of a few acres or less.

Standing water and Georgia evenings bring bugs out at dusk, so the porch people actually sit on after dark is a screened porch. How a screened room differs from a sunroom, and what each gives up in airflow, is set out there.

Retaining walls in Alpharetta: 205 feet of fall inside the city

Sampled across the city, Alpharetta's ground runs from 959 feet above sea level to 1,164.

Fall like that shows up one lot at a time, as a back yard that drops away from the house or a bank beside the driveway. A wall is what turns a slope into level ground you can use, and how one is built to hold is covered under retaining walls.

Patios and walkways in Alpharetta: Big Creek, Foe Killer Creek, Long Indian Creek
