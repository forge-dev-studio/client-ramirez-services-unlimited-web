Ten road miles from the shop

18,183 people live in Calhoun across 7,199 homes, 3,703 of them owner occupied, and the whole city is 10.3 road miles away up the interstate. That distance is the argument. Nothing else on this page needs to carry as much weight, because for a small job the difference between ten miles and forty is whether it gets scheduled this month.

A 1986 median and the second-generation deck

Half the houses here predate 1986. That vintage is old enough that the original deck has usually been dealt with once and young enough that the second one is now the problem. What tends to survive is the footing layout, which means a rebuild can often reuse the geometry even when nothing above ground is worth keeping. The deck build page covers how that is framed, and staining and sealing is what decides whether the next one lasts as long.

Fence runs in a mid-sized place

Calhoun is a mid-sized incorporated place of 7,199 housing units, which puts it between subdivision grid work and acreage runs, and what that means for gates and run length is on the Calhoun fence page, with the materials covered by the fence company page.

55.8 percent, and what a shade structure has to prove

Calhoun sits at 55.8 percent owner occupied, a little under the footprint middle. In a market like that a pergola is competing with the idea of doing nothing, so what sells it is not the structure but the shade line: where the shadow actually falls at four in the afternoon in July, which is a question of orientation and rafter spacing rather than of style.

The Oostanaula River and Moss Lake

Two named waters sit inside the Calhoun boundary, the Oostanaula River and Moss Lake. Standing water is the one that drives insect pressure hardest, and Moss Lake is standing water. Anywhere within reach of it a screened enclosure buys back the hours between six and nine at night, which for most of the summer is the only part of the day worth being outside for.

251 feet of grade, the gentlest of the northern hubs

Calhoun runs 619 to 870 feet, a spread of 251 feet. That is modest by the standards of Dalton or Cartersville, and it changes the wall conversation: the typical Calhoun job is a low garden wall squaring off a soft slope rather than a structural hold. The retaining wall page covers both, and the shorter one is usually the honest answer here.

Blackwood Creek, Lynn Creek and Oothcaloga Creek

Five named streams cross the boundary, including Blackwood Creek, Lynn Creek and Oothcaloga Creek. A yard sitting between two of them has water crossing it twice, and every hard surface you add changes where that water ends up. Patios and walkways here are planned with the fall first and the pattern second.

Do you come to Calhoun for small repairs?

Yes. At 10.3 road miles it is the cheapest drive on the board after Adairsville, so a gate that will not latch or a bad stair tread is worth the trip.

Can a new deck reuse the old footings?

Sometimes. It depends on where they are, how they were poured and what the new frame needs to span. It is one of the first things worth checking, because it changes the whole job.
