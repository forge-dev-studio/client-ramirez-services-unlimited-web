10,161 homes and two kinds of lot

Cartersville carries 10,161 housing units as a large incorporated place, and the useful thing about that figure is what it hides. The older part of the city was platted long before anybody was drawing cul-de-sacs, so the lots there are irregular, the boundaries do not always meet at right angles, and a fence has to be laid out rather than simply measured. The newer subdivisions are the opposite: predictable rectangles, standard side yards, and a run length you can estimate over the phone.

A 1990 median and a genuine material choice

Housing here has a 1990 median, which puts a lot of original fences at the point where they are neither fine nor finished. That is the moment people actually compare materials rather than defaulting, so it is worth the comparison: wood privacy for screening and cost, or vinyl and aluminum where the maintenance is the deciding factor. Each fails differently and neither is the right answer everywhere.

Slope, and why a Cartersville fence steps

This is the steepest place in Bartow County, and a fence crossing falling ground has to either follow the grade or step down it. Following the grade keeps a continuous line and works with wood. Stepping keeps every panel level and leaves triangles at the bottom that either get filled or do not. Which one suits depends on how fast the ground drops, and it is a decision worth making before anything is ordered rather than on the day.

Should a fence follow the slope or step down it?

Following the slope suits wood and keeps a clean top line. Stepping suits panels and preformed sections and leaves gaps at the bottom that need filling. How steep the ground is decides it.
