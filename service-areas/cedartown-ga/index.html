Polk County

Deck Builder in Cedartown, GA

Old housing, low ownership and two lakes inside the city line. Cedartown doesn't look like anywhere else we work.

Call (706) 879-8873 Get a free estimate

About 58 minutes, 37.4 road miles by New Calhoun Highway Northeast and Cedartown Highway.

10,227 people in housing that mostly predates 1974

Four thousand homes, and fewer than half of them lived in by the people who own them. Like Rome, half of Cedartown was finished before 1974. That's old housing in a town where most people rent, and it makes for a particular kind of work.

Boards that have earned their retirement

Half the houses here were finished before 1974. Any deck that came with the house has been replaced, patched or abandoned by now, and the ones still standing are usually held up by framing that was never designed for the way it has been loaded since. The starting question is whether there's anything worth keeping, which is what deck repair and replacement is for, and the railing and stair is nearly always the part that fails a safety look first.

4,050 homes on older lots

Cedartown's 4,050 homes sit on older ground with irregular lot shapes, not modern subdivision grid, and what that does to a fence line is the subject of the Cedartown fence page, with materials on the fence company page.

43.3 percent owner occupied, the lowest of them all

Fewer than half of occupied Cedartown homes are owner occupied. That makes it the thinnest pergola and patio cover market we serve, and we're not going to dress that up. Where the work does happen it's in the settled streets, and it tends to be a cover over an existing slab, not a new structure on new ground.

Vance Lake and Springdale Lake

Two named lakes sit inside the Cedartown boundary. Both are still water, which is the kind that drives insect pressure hardest, so a screened porch here's doing real work instead of adding a feature. On an older house the porch structure often already exists and only needs screening properly, which is the cheapest of the three routes on that page.

197 feet across the boundary

Cedartown runs 766 to 963 feet, 197 feet of change and the third flattest ground we build on. Flat and old is a patio and deck-replacement profile, not a wall profile, and most Cedartown lots don't need a retaining wall at all. Where one does go in it is usually low and decorative.

Cedar Creek and Lake Creek

Only two named streams cross the boundary. On flat ground with few defined channels, water leaves slowly, and a hard surface put down without a deliberate fall will hold it. Getting the slope right is most of the job on a Cedartown patio or walkway.

Fire pits in Polk County

It's a site decision, made standing in the yard with the seating worked out first. The build sits with hardscape and the layouts are in the fire pit guide.
