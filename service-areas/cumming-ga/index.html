Lake Alice, and how little of it there's

The only still water inside Cumming is Lake Alice, and it covers a hundredth of a square mile. That's a pond. What does underdecking actually do?

It catches what comes through the boards, so the space beneath a raised deck stays dry enough to use. It's worth deciding on before the frame goes up, not after.

Can a fire pit go in close to a deck?
