McDonough and Stockbridge

Decks & Fences in Henry County, GA

McDonough's median house dates to 2006, which makes it an add-to county rather than a rebuild one.

Stockbridge is about 102 minutes at 84 road miles, McDonough about 109 at 90.4, both down I-75.

13,996 owner-occupied homes, and young ones

McDonough and Stockbridge hold 26,544 homes between them and 13,996 owner-occupied households. The median McDonough house dates to 2006 and the median Stockbridge house to 1998, so this is young housing stock. Young stock changes the job: the frame is generally sound and the conversation is about adding something rather than replacing what failed.

An add-to county, not a rebuild county

On a house built in 2006 the original deck, if there is one, is usually still structurally fine. So the work here runs toward extending it, screening it in, or putting proper railings and stairs on something that was built to the minimum. It also runs toward first-time builds on houses that never had a deck at all, which is the more common Henry job. Composite decking is worth pricing against wood on stock this new, because the house will outlast the first surface either way.

Gentle ground, 166 to 196 feet

Relief runs 166 feet at McDonough and 196 at Stockbridge, which is a moderate, consistent band with no real extremes. That's straightforward ground for a patio or a walkway, and it's why Henry work leans toward flat hardscape more than the retaining walls that dominate the northern counties.

The far end of the drive

Henry is the longest run we make, over an hour and a half each way. That is the honest constraint, and it means we group Henry work rather than take it one job at a time. A small repair down there generally isn't worth what the drive adds to it, and we'll say so. A deck, a fence line or a concrete pour is an ordinary day. Send the details and we'll tell you quickly.
