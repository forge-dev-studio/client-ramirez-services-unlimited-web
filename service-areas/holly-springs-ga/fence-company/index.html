7,312 homes, nearly all of them recent

Holly Springs is a large incorporated place with 7,312 housing units and a housing median of 2009, the newest of any town we cover. Almost nothing here has an old fence to take down, which removes a whole category of cost and adds a different one: on a new subdivision lot the grading was finished for the builder rather than for you, and the line you want to fence may run across ground that was recently moved.

2009 housing, and the clean sheet

A first fence is the only time the choice is genuinely unconstrained. Nothing has to match, nothing has to tie in, and the whole run can be one thing. That is the moment to compare wood privacy against vinyl and aluminum on the terms that actually matter to you, which are usually how much maintenance you will realistically do and how long you plan to be there.

Settling ground and what it does to a new fence

Fill placed during construction keeps compacting for years afterward. A post set into recently placed fill can be perfect on the day and out of plumb two winters later, through nobody's fault. On a new-build lot it is worth knowing which parts of the yard are cut and which are fill before the holes are dug, because the answer changes how deep they go.
