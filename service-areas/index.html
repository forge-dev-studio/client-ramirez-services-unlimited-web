Where the drive stops

Past about an hour the math stops working.

A crew that spends three hours a day in a truck is a crew that is not building anything. You pay for that whether it is itemized or not.

So the honest limit is roughly an hour of real driving from Adairsville, measured on the road rather than across the map.

What we do when we get there

The same work everywhere: decks, fencing, pergolas and patio covers, screened porches, retaining walls, patios and walkways, and fire pits.

What changes by town is the ground.

Housing age decides how many decks are due for replacement. Slope decides whether a wall comes before the patio.

The town pages carry those facts, one at a time.
