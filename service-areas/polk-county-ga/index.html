Polk County sits in a 56 to 58 minute band, between 33.9 and 37.4 road miles west of Adairsville.

3,280 owner-occupied homes and a lot of history

Polk contributes 6,932 housing units and 3,280 owner-occupied households across three places. It is a small county by household count and an unusual one by age, and the age is the whole story.

1958 to 1974, older than anywhere else measured

Rockmart shows a 1958 housing median and Aragon 1960. Those are the two oldest figures in the entire market file, and Cedartown at 1974 is the youngest place in the county. On housing that old the deck question is rarely about the deck: it is about what the deck is attached to and what it is standing on. That is why repair and replacement is the right entry point here and sealing is what protects whatever gets built next.

One page, two named towns

Cedartown carries the county page. Rockmart, at 1,234 owner-occupied homes, and Aragon, at 431, sit below the threshold for a page each and are named here instead. Rockmart is 36.6 road miles out and Aragon 33.9, so both are actually closer to Adairsville than Cedartown is. Neither is a lesser service area. Both are simply too small for an honest page of their own.

132 to 231 feet, the flattest county on the list

Relief across Polk runs 132 feet at Aragon to 231 feet at Rockmart, with Cedartown at 197. That band is the lowest of any county here, so Polk is not a retaining wall county and this page will not sell it as one. Flat and old points at deck replacement and at ground-level hardscape instead.

Two lakes in Cedartown, nothing named in the other two

Vance Lake and Springdale Lake sit inside the Cedartown boundary. Rockmart and Aragon carry no named water at all, which is a real result rather than a gap, and it means the screened porch case in those two towns rests on ordinary summer use rather than on a lake. Euharlee Creek runs through both of them and Cedar Creek and Lake Creek through Cedartown, which is what patio and walkway layouts have to account for.

Three small and mid-sized incorporated places

Every Polk place is incorporated and none is large. That produces older, less regular platting and fence lines that follow the shape of a lot rather than a grid, so the fence work here needs more layout time and fewer standard panels than a Cherokee County subdivision does. The county seat gets that in full on the Cedartown fence page.

Ownership from 43 to 62 percent

Cedartown at 43.3 percent is the lowest ownership share of any hub, while Rockmart runs 61.9 and Aragon 60.9. The two smaller towns are considerably more settled than the county seat, which is where the patio cover work in this county actually is.

No fire pit claim, no photographs yet

The record supports no county-level statement about fire pits, and the build belongs with hardscape in any case. Nothing in Polk County has been photographed for this site. See the gallery for work that has, the service areas page for the whole footprint, or get in touch.
