6,965 homes on the flattest ground we work on

Powder Springs is flat. There are 122 feet between the lowest ground in town and the highest, less than anywhere else we work, and the 6,965 homes here sit on lots with room around them. What comes of that is long, straight, level runs with very few corners and a genuine reason to fit a drive gate, not only a walk gate.

What long runs are made of

The older half of Powder Springs was finished before 1996, so the first fences are at the replacement point. On a long run the material choice is driven by arithmetic, not taste, because every extra foot multiplies. Wood privacy stays the value answer where screening matters, and chain link remains hard to beat where the job is containment across a large yard and nobody needs to be hidden from anybody.

Drive gates are a different animal

A walk gate is three feet of hardware. A drive gate is ten or twelve feet of unsupported span that has to stay square through every season, and it either gets built as two leaves or gets a wheel. Posts on either side of it carry loads no line post ever sees, and on flat open ground there's nothing to brace against except what gets put in the hole. It's the part of a Powder Springs fence that most deserves the attention.
