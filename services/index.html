Why these sit together

These are not separate trades subcontracted out under one name. They are the things one crew with a saw, a post hole digger and a plate compactor can build in a back yard.

That is why they sit together. A deck usually arrives with steps, a rail and a gate. A patio usually arrives with a wall holding the ground back behind it.

What each page covers

Each page explains the job itself: how it is built, what it is made of, and what actually decides between the options.

Prices are not on them. Cost is a guide question and it lives in the guides, where there is room to say what a number depends on instead of printing one and hoping.
